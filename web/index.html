    Documentation

    How CardioLens Works

    CardioLens combines a 400-tree calibrated Random Forest classifier with local Shapley Additive Explanations (Tree SHAP).

    Explainability Principles

    Tree SHAP Additivity: The sum of all 13 factor contributions plus the base expectation exactly equals the model score for every prediction.

    Clinical Grouping: One-hot encoded categorical columns are mathematically recombined back into their original 13 clinical factors so outputs are interpretable to medical practitioners.

    Dual Decision Cut-offs: Predictions are classified into Lower (< 0.30), Moderate (0.30 to 0.50), and Higher (≥ 0.50) using operating thresholds optimized for high clinical sensitivity.

    Methodology

    Model Details & Validation

    Rigorous 5-fold Stratified Group Cross-Validation trained on de-duplicated patient records.

    Model Specifications

    • Architecture: Random Forest Classifier (400 estimators, max_features='sqrt')
    • Cross-Validation ROC AUC: 0.902 across 5 stratified folds
    • Test Sensitivity / Recall: 85.3% at 0.30 operating point
    • Data Deduplication: 723 duplicate rows purged; evaluated on 302 unique patients

    Limitations & Inferred Encodings

    Labels for chest pain type, resting ECG, ST slope and thallium test were inferred by matching the dataset's value counts and disease rates to the published Cleveland data; the Kaggle file does not document its encoding.
